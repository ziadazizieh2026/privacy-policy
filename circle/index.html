Privacy Policy

Application: Circle

Developer: Althorya Education

Effective Date: September 9, 2026

Introduction

Circle is an educational application designed to help students learn mathematics and analytic geometry, with a focus on circles and related concepts.

Information Collection

Circle does not collect, store, or transmit personal information to the developer. Mathematical calculations are performed locally on the user's device.

In-App Purchases

Circle may offer optional in-app purchases through Google Play Billing. Payments are processed securely by Google Play. The developer does not receive or store users' payment information.

Data Sharing

Circle does not sell, rent, or share users' personal information with third parties.

Children's Privacy

Circle does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

Circle does not maintain developer servers for storing users' personal information.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.

Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer using the contact email provided on the Circle Google Play store listing.
