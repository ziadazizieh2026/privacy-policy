Privacy Policy

Application: Line

Developer: Althorya Education

Effective Date: September 9, 2026

Introduction

Line is an educational application designed to help students learn mathematics and analytic geometry, with a focus on straight lines, equations of lines, and related concepts.

Information Collection

Line does not collect, store, or transmit personal information to the developer. Mathematical calculations are performed locally on the user's device.

In-App Purchases

Line may offer optional in-app purchases through Google Play Billing. Payments are processed securely by Google Play. The developer does not receive or store users' payment information.

Data Sharing

Line does not sell, rent, or share users' personal information with third parties.

Children's Privacy

Line does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

Line does not maintain developer servers for storing users' personal information.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.

Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer using the contact email provided on the Line Google Play store listing.
